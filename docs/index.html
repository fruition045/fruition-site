Building with hemp

Fruition is working to bring hemp building to Huntsville. At the center of it is hempcrete, a wall material that is better for the people who build with it, the families who live in it, and the land it comes from.

Hempcrete is a mix of the chopped inner core of the hemp stalk, a lime binder, and water. It doesn't carry weight on its own, so it's packed around a regular wood frame. That one wall then does the work of siding, sheathing, insulation, and drywall.

Resists fire, mold, and pests
A hempcrete wall is long lasting and needs little upkeep.
Keeps the inside comfortable
The wall stores heat and soaks up and releases moisture, which can cut heating and cooling bills sharply.
No toxic chemicals
Safer for the crews who install it and the families who live with it.
Very little waste
Next to no job-site waste, and the wall can be broken down and recycled at the end of its life.
Pulls carbon from the air
Hemp absorbs CO₂ as it grows, and the lime takes CO₂ back in as it cures and hardens toward stone.
Keeps money local
Local farmers can grow the hemp, local shops can process it, and local builders can install it.
