Privacy

Last updated November 25, 2026

This policy covers thefruitionfoundation.org, the website of Fruition Charitable Arts Foundation and Services.

What this website collects

Nothing. This website has no forms, no accounts, no cookies, and no analytics or advertising trackers. Its fonts are served from this website, not from a third party.

When you email us

If you email info@thefruitionfoundation.org, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply to you, and we do not sell or share it.

Our hosting provider

Like any website host, our hosting provider may keep short-lived technical logs (such as IP addresses) to deliver pages and protect against abuse.

Clyde

Clyde, our history app, has its own privacy policy at askclyde.us/privacy.
